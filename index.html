GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Are these QR codes free to use?

Yes. You can create and download QR codes without creating an account.

Do my details get uploaded?

No. QR codes are generated locally in your browser. Your entered content is not sent to our server.

Will my QR code expire?

The QR code itself does not expire. If it points to a website, that destination must remain available.

Which file formats can I download?

Download a PNG for everyday use or an SVG for sharp scaling in design and print projects.
